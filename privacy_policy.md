## Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 12, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how **Tune It All** ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in the **Tune It All** mobile application (the "App").

### 1. No Data Collection

We believe in your right to privacy. **Tune It All** does not collect, store, or transmit any Personal Data.

* **No Accounts:** You do not need to create an account or provide an email address to use the App.
* **No Tracking:** We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or any third-party analytics (such as Google Analytics or Firebase) to monitor your behavior.
* **No Backend:** All app logic happens locally on your device. We do not maintain any servers or databases that store your information.

### 2. Permissions and Local Data

The App requires specific iOS permissions to function (such as access to your Microphone).

* These permissions are used **strictly for the App's functionality** on your device.
* Any data accessed through these permissions stays on your device and is never uploaded to us or any third party.

### 3. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with third-party advertising networks or social media APIs.

### 4. Children’s Privacy

Since we do not collect any personal information, our App does not knowingly or unknowingly collect data from children under the age of 13.

### 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since the App does not collect user contact information, we recommend checking this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately upon being posted within the App Store or the App itself.
